About

让 AI 造福人类，让机器人走进千家万户。

Using AI to benefit humanity and bring robots into everyday homes.

目前我在澳门大学 MohismLab，在李庆标教授和Joel Oliveira Reis 教授的指导下参与机器人与 Agent 开发。

I develop robotics and agent systems with MohismLab at the University of Macau, under the guidance of Prof. Qingbiao Li and Prof. Joel Oliveira Reis.

世界的变化，常常来自很多普通人认真做好的小事情。我喜欢研究能够减少重复劳动、提高工作效率的 AI 工具和工作流，也关注如何在工程层面优化 Agent 能力，以及如何提升机器人的空间感知能力。始终坚信，AI 应该帮助人类走向更健康、更安全、更高质量的生活。永远相信，美好的事情即将发生。

World-changing progress often comes from ordinary people doing small things carefully. I enjoy researching AI tools and workflows that reduce repetitive work and improve productivity. I am also interested in optimizing agent capabilities at the engineering level and improving robots' spatial perception. I have always believed that AI should help humanity move toward a healthier, safer and higher quality of life. Always believe that something wonderful is about to happen.
