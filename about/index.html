About

让 AI 造福人类，让机器人走进千家万户。

Using AI to benefit humanity and bring robots into everyday homes.

I work on bringing robotics and AI systems into the real world. I am committed to using AI to benefit humanity and bring robots into everyday homes, while valuing small things that can change the world.

目前我在澳门大学 MohismLab 开展机器人与 Agent 相关的研究。

I currently conduct research on robotics and agents at MohismLab, University of Macau.

相比追逐宏大的概念，我更在意技术能否解决真实问题，致力于探索 AI 造福人类的方式。

More than grand concepts, I care about whether technology can solve real problems, and I am committed to exploring ways for AI to benefit humanity.

对人真正有用

Useful to people

技术不该只是漂亮的指标，而应该让人更从容、更有选择。

Technology should not be a distant metric. It should give people more ease and more choices.

把时间还给生活

Return time to life

让工具处理重复和琐碎，把精力留给创造、关系与生活。

Let tools handle repetition and friction, leaving energy for creation, relationships and life.

做改变世界的小事

Small things that matter

世界的变化，常常来自很多普通人认真做好的小事情。

World-changing progress is often made from many ordinary people doing small things carefully.

我希望自己做的东西，最终都能回到一个朴素的问题：它是否真的让某个人的生活变得更好一点？

I hope everything I make returns to one simple question: did it genuinely improve someone's life?
