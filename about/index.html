Small Changes Matter
About Simon

希望用 AI，让生活和工作更轻松一点。

Using AI to make life and work a little lighter.

我是 Simon。比起追逐宏大的概念，我更在意那些具体、真实、能被人感受到的改变。

I am Simon. More than grand concepts, I care about changes that are concrete, real and felt by people.

我做 AI、机器人和 Agent 相关的事情，也喜欢读书、写作和整理工具。最想坚持的方向很简单：让 AI 帮助人更好地生活和工作，为更多人带来实际的便利和善意，也认真做一些能够改变世界的小事。

I work across AI, robotics and agents, and I enjoy reading, writing and refining tools. The direction I keep returning to is simple: help AI improve how people live and work, create practical value and goodwill, and keep doing small things that can change the world.

对人真正有用

Useful to people

技术不该只是漂亮的指标，而应该让人更从容、更有选择。

Technology should not be a distant metric. It should give people more ease and more choices.

把时间还给生活

Return time to life

让工具处理重复和琐碎，把精力留给创造、关系与生活。

Let tools handle repetition and friction, leaving energy for creation, relationships and life.

做改变世界的小事

Small things that matter

世界的变化，常常来自很多普通人认真做好的小事情。

World-changing progress is often made from many ordinary people doing small things carefully.

我希望自己做的东西，最终都能回到一个朴素的问题：它是否真的让某个人的生活变得更好一点？

I hope everything I make returns to one simple question: did it genuinely improve someone's life?
