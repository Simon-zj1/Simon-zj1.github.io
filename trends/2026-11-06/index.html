SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization

SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization

2611.08483 · cs.AI · 2026-11-052611.08483 · cs.AI · 2026-11-05

论文针对的是 LLM 引导的进化搜索每轮都生成长篇思维链，导致程序优化慢且多样性不足。SwarmEvolve 在每轮让数百个低成本 Agent 在潜空间推理、不解码成文本，并直接传递 KV cache 共享信息；新颖性过滤器用隐藏向量距离保留不同思路，汇总 Agent 再组合各 swarm 的缓存。它在 21 个数学发现与科学计算问题上取得最高平均分，并以 3–8 分钟达到其他 SOTA 需 60 分钟才达到的最佳成绩，Qwen3 8B 在若干题上超过 AlphaEvolve 早期用前沿模型找到的解。限制是潜空间通信绑定具体模型接口，跨模型迁移与可解释性仍需验证。

The paper targets LLM-guided evolutionary search spending every round on long text reasoning, slowing optimisation and reducing population diversity. SwarmEvolve runs hundreds of low-cost agents per round in latent space, without decoding to text, and shares KV caches; a novelty filter preserves distinct hidden-state ideas and a summariser combines swarm caches. On 21 mathematical-discovery and scientific-computing problems it achieves the highest average score and reaches other SOTA results in 3–8 minutes instead of 60, while Qwen3 8B beats some early AlphaEvolve solutions. The limitation is dependence on model-specific latent interfaces, with cross-model transfer and interpretability still unverified.

The first 1,000 verified environments see frontier AI scientists outperform the human researchers 74% of the time, while 6,400 hours of research traces train a small model to improve research ideation by 158% and approach frontier models. The limitation is that reproduction quality, missing implementation detail and evaluation boundaries can bias the comparison.

OopsieRecover: Learning Transferable VLA Corrections from Semantic Interventions

OopsieRecover: Learning Transferable VLA Corrections from Semantic Interventions

2611.08462 · cs.RO · 2026-11-052611.08462 · cs.RO · 2026-11-05

论文针对的是VLA 机器人在部署中会失败，而 DAgger 式纠正数据要几十到上百次交互，且难迁移到新物体和场景。OopsieRecover 用 abduction 从少量纠正演示推断失败意图，把知识编码为稠密空间表示和条件代价，在执行时惩罚不良区域、引导策略进入可行动区域。它在六类任务及物体姿态、场景与相关任务变化中，用少量纠正数据跨新情况恢复，而无需为每个变化重采数据。限制是可解释性与安全仍依赖推断出的语义是否正确，论文规模也较小。

The paper targets VLA robots failing in deployment while DAgger-style corrections need tens to hundreds of interactions and generalise poorly to new objects or scenes. OopsieRecover uses abduction to infer the intent behind a handful of corrective demonstrations, encoding it as dense spatial representations and conditional costs that penalise bad behaviour and steer the policy into desirable action regions. Across six tasks and changes in pose, scene and related tasks, a few corrections transfer to novel situations without collecting new data for every variation. The limitation is that safety and interpretability depend on the inferred semantics being correct, and the study covers a relatively small set of tasks.
