The paper targets live evaluation of forecasting agents mixing retrieval with reasoning, while retrospective searches leak later answers. It places short value statements from Schwartz's theory of basic values, covering openness to change, conservation, self-enhancement and self-transcendence, into the agent's persistent instruction file and holds them fixed across multiple improvement iterations.
