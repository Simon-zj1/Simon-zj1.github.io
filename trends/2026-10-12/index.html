    To Cache or Not to Cache: Understanding and Mitigating Safety Incompatibility in Cross-Model KV Cache Reuse

    To Cache or Not to Cache: Understanding and Mitigating Safety Incompatibility in Cross-Model KV Cache Reuse

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：评测、multi-agent）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: 评测, multi-agent)

    这篇论文盯上了 KV 缓存共享的一个盲区：跨模型复用前缀缓存能省下 prefill 计算，但既有方法只优化延迟与生成质量，安全性影响没人系统评估[29]。作者把「接收模型的安全行为偏离其原生表现」定义为安全不兼容（safety incompatibility），并在多种「发送方—接收方」组合与复用配置上做了系统评测。结论有两个要点：不兼容的方向与幅度取决于模型配对与复用配置；而在所有评估配对中，复用 value 张量造成的偏离都显著大于复用 key。据此提出 VReparo：离线学习逐层、逐 KV head 的仿射映射，把复用的发送方 value 张量对齐到接收方原生分布，再解码；在两组模型配对的全量复用与当前最优的选择性复用设置下，它降低了 HarmBench 的平均攻击成功率。对做多 Agent 与推理加速的团队，参考价值是缓存复用是一项安全决策，不只是性能优化，必须按模型对单独评测；限制是缓解依赖逐模型对离线训练映射，规模扩大后的维护成本与未知配对的泛化尚未验证。

    This paper targets a blind spot in KV-cache sharing: cross-model prefix reuse saves prefill compute, but existing methods optimise only latency and generation quality while the safety implications go unevaluated[29]. The authors define safety incompatibility as deviation from the receiver's native safety behaviour and evaluate it systematically across sender-receiver pairs and reuse configurations. Two findings stand out: the direction and magnitude of incompatibility depend on the model pair and reuse configuration, and value reuse causes substantially larger deviations than key reuse across all evaluated pairs. They propose VReparo, which learns per-layer, per-KV-head affine maps offline to align reused sender value tensors with receiver-native counterparts before decoding, reducing average HarmBench attack success under full KV reuse and the state-of-the-art selective reuse setting. For multi-agent and inference-acceleration teams the reference is that cache reuse is a safety decision, not just a performance one, and must be evaluated per model pair; the limit is offline per-pair training and untested generalisation to unseen pairs.

    边界：限制是缓解依赖逐模型对离线训练映射，规模扩大后的维护成本与未知配对的泛化尚未验证。

    Limits: the limit is offline per-pair training and untested generalisation to unseen pairs.

    RSIPO: Recursive Self-Improving Policy Optimization for Agentic Reinforcement Learning via Skill-Policy Co-Evolution

    RSIPO: Recursive Self-Improving Policy Optimization for Agentic Reinforcement Learning via Skill-Policy Co-Evolution

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：上下文、context）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: 上下文, context)

    RSIPO 针对 Agent 强化学习里「技能」被静态使用的问题：近期做法把可验证奖励的 RL（RLVR）与在线自蒸馏（OPSD）结合，用带特权上下文（如 Agent 技能）的教师模型为学生生成的 token 打分；但这些方法基本忽略了技能的在线演化与教师监督的可靠性[30]。做法上提出递归自改进策略优化（RSIPO）：让 Agent 技能与策略模型在训练过程中共同进化——策略自己从已完成轨迹中抽取技能，并通过轨迹回放显式优化其技能生成能力，同时用经验贝叶斯收缩动态校准教师监督的权重。对做 Agent 后训练的团队，参考价值是技能库不应当是静态资产，而应与策略一同迭代，并对教师信号的可信度做动态加权；限制是摘要未给出具体基准与提升幅度，技能抽取的质量上限与「技能—策略共同漂移」的风险需要复现验证。

    RSIPO targets the static use of skills in agentic RL: recent work combines RL with verifiable rewards (RLVR) and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD), using a teacher conditioned on privileged context such as agentic skills to score the student's tokens, but largely overlooks the on-policy evolution of those skills and the reliability of teacher supervision[30]. The method, Recursive Self-Improving Policy Optimization, lets agentic skills and the policy model co-evolve during training — the policy extracts skills from completed trajectories itself and explicitly optimises its skill-generation capability through trajectory replay, while empirical Bayes shrinkage dynamically calibrates teacher supervision. For agent post-training teams the reference is that a skill library should iterate alongside the policy, with teacher signals weighted by measured reliability; the limit is that no benchmarks or effect sizes are given, leaving skill-extraction ceilings and co-drift risk to reproduce.

    边界：限制是摘要未给出具体基准与提升幅度，技能抽取的质量上限与「技能—策略共同漂移」的风险需要复现验证。
