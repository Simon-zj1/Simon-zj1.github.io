    TurnCost: Turns, Not Observations: Cache-Aware Loop Engineering for Coding Agents

    TurnCost: Turns, Not Observations: Cache-Aware Loop Engineering for Coding Agents

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：上下文、context）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: 上下文, context)

    TurnCost 把「编码 Agent 的循环设计」（作者称为 loop engineering，涵盖上下文管理、模型选择、压缩与终止）当作可量化的成本问题，并检查现有优化方向是否真的有效[29]。它要解决的是成本归因错误：社区普遍认为长构建日志是主要成本来源，但对 600 条公开编码 Agent 轨迹（两套 scaffold、两个模型）的分析显示，观测的中位长度只有 118–286 token，那些被拿来论证压缩必要性的超大日志出现在第 99 百分位附近；更关键的是 98.6% 的输入 token 由前缀缓存提供，因此在近乎理想缓存下，按「平铺定价」估算会平均高估循环成本约 7 倍。作者据此推导并验证了「把某些轮次委派给小模型」的盈亏平衡条件，在评估的半数设置里委派反而增加成本，与预测的边界接近；并且轮次数量能解释对数轨迹成本的 84%，而平均观测长度只能解释 4%。对做编码 Agent 的团队，参考价值是先测缓存命中率与轮次数量，再决定优化方向——多数团队该优化的是「少跑几轮」而不是「压日志」；限制是结论来自公开轨迹与特定 scaffold，缓存策略不同结果会变。

    TurnCost treats coding-agent loop design — which the authors call loop engineering, covering context management, model selection, compaction and termination — as a quantifiable cost problem and checks whether the usual optimisations actually work[29]. It addresses misattributed cost: the community assumes long build logs dominate, yet across 600 public coding-agent trajectories spanning two scaffolds and two models, median observations hold only 118–286 tokens, while the huge logs used to justify compression sit near the 99th percentile; more importantly 98.6% of input tokens are served from a prefix cache, so flat input pricing overestimates loop cost by roughly 7x on average under near-ideal caching. The paper derives and validates a break-even condition for delegating turns to cheaper models, finding delegation increases cost in half of the evaluated settings, near the predicted boundary, and that turn count explains 84% of the variance in log trajectory cost versus 4% for mean observation length. For coding agent teams the reference is to measure cache hit rate and turn count before choosing optimisations — most teams should target fewer turns, not smaller logs; the limit is that results come from public trajectories and specific scaffolds.

    边界：作者据此推导并验证了「把某些轮次委派给小模型」的盈亏平衡条件，**在评估的半数设置里委派反而增加成本**，与预测的边界接近；

    Limits: the limit is that results come from public trajectories and specific scaffolds.

    Trace Evolution Bench: Measuring Whether Agents Improve from Their Own Environment Traces

    Trace Evolution Bench: Measuring Whether Agents Improve from Their Own Environment Traces

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：上下文、context、评测、benchmark）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: 上下文, context, 评测, benchmark)

    这篇论文指出自进化 Agent 的评测存在方法学漏洞：常用指标是「更新后的留出成功率减去更新前」，但这个差值可能只是把「冻结的重新打分」当成了进化[30]。做法上提出 Trace Evolution Bench（TEB），把重新打分与真正的上下文或权重更新分开，并给出三个冻结的 80/20/40 数据包；只有验证集不下降、留出标识符绝不进入更新、且文本更新不与权重更新混排时，才接受一次更新；主榜是编码任务，由封闭工作区里的隐藏单元测试评分。结果很扎实：在该数据包上（40 个留出任务、单一模型族），DeepSeek v4 Pro 在 no_evolve 条件下策略完全不变，却在三个随机种子上分别移动了 7.5、5.0 与 12.5 个百分点——这就是评测噪声的量级。进一步，在受 GEPA、ACE 与 ExpeL 启发的九个文本更新单元中，七个超过该种子自身的波动带，其中一个 GEPA 种子反而下降了一个波动带的宽度（-12.5，回归率 0.48）；GLM 与 MiniMax 无法复现这个「九分之七」的模式，没有任何方法在三个模型的所有种子上都稳定高于自身波动带。对做自进化研究的团队，参考价值是报告进化增益时必须同时给出「不更新时的种子波动」，否则无法区分学习与噪声；限制是结论基于单一基准族与三款模型，跨任务族的波动幅度需要另行测量。

    This paper identifies a methodological hole in self-evolving agent evaluation: the usual metric is held-out success after an update minus before, and that difference can credit a frozen rescore as evolution[30]. It introduces Trace Evolution Bench (TEB), separating rescoring from a genuine context or weight update, with three frozen 80/20/40 packs; an update is accepted only when validation does not fall, held-out identifiers never enter the update, and text updates are not ranked against weight updates; the primary scoreboard is coding, graded by hidden unit tests in a closed workspace. The results are sobering: on that pack (40 held-out tasks, one model family), DeepSeek v4 Pro under no_evolve never changes its policy yet moves 7.5, 5.0 and 12.5 percentage points across three seeds — that is the scale of evaluation noise. Further, of nine text-update cells inspired by GEPA, ACE and ExpeL, seven exceed that seed's own band, while one GEPA seed falls by a full band width (-12.5, regression 0.48); GLM and MiniMax do not reproduce the seven-of-nine pattern, and no method stays above its own band on every seed across all three models. For self-evolution researchers the reference is to always report the no-update seed band alongside claimed gains. The limit is one benchmark family and three models.

    边界：限制是结论基于单一基准族与三款模型，跨任务族的波动幅度需要另行测量。

    Limits: The limit is one benchmark family and three models.

    来源：Sources: arXiv

dscode is a DeepSeek coding-agent harness featuring a persistent shell, Ultra subagents, auto approval, Chrome MCP and session telemetry[28]. TurnCost: Turns, Not Observations: Cache-Aware Loop Engineering for Coding Agents

TurnCost: Turns, Not Observations: Cache-Aware Loop Engineering for Coding Agents

2610.15093 · cs.SE, cs.AI · 2026-10-102610.15093 · cs.SE, cs.AI · 2026-10-10

TurnCost 把「编码 Agent 的循环设计」（作者称为 loop engineering，涵盖上下文管理、模型选择、压缩与终止）当作可量化的成本问题，并检查现有优化方向是否真的有效[29]。它要解决的是成本归因错误：社区普遍认为长构建日志是主要成本来源，但对 600 条公开编码 Agent 轨迹（两套 scaffold、两个模型）的分析显示，观测的中位长度只有 118–286 token，那些被拿来论证压缩必要性的超大日志出现在第 99 百分位附近；更关键的是 98.6% 的输入 token 由前缀缓存提供，因此在近乎理想缓存下，按「平铺定价」估算会平均高估循环成本约 7 倍。作者据此推导并验证了「把某些轮次委派给小模型」的盈亏平衡条件，在评估的半数设置里委派反而增加成本，与预测的边界接近；并且轮次数量能解释对数轨迹成本的 84%，而平均观测长度只能解释 4%。对做编码 Agent 的团队，参考价值是先测缓存命中率与轮次数量，再决定优化方向——多数团队该优化的是「少跑几轮」而不是「压日志」；限制是结论来自公开轨迹与特定 scaffold，缓存策略不同结果会变。

TurnCost treats coding-agent loop design — which the authors call loop engineering, covering context management, model selection, compaction and termination — as a quantifiable cost problem and checks whether the usual optimisations actually work[29]. It addresses misattributed cost: the community assumes long build logs dominate, yet across 600 public coding-agent trajectories spanning two scaffolds and two models, median observations hold only 118–286 tokens, while the huge logs used to justify compression sit near the 99th percentile; more importantly 98.6% of input tokens are served from a prefix cache, so flat input pricing overestimates loop cost by roughly 7x on average under near-ideal caching. The paper derives and validates a break-even condition for delegating turns to cheaper models, finding delegation increases cost in half of the evaluated settings, near the predicted boundary, and that turn count explains 84% of the variance in log trajectory cost versus 4% for mean observation length. For coding agent teams the reference is to measure cache hit rate and turn count before choosing optimisations — most teams should target fewer turns, not smaller logs; the limit is that results come from public trajectories and specific scaffolds.

Trace Evolution Bench: Measuring Whether Agents Improve from Their Own Environment Traces

Trace Evolution Bench: Measuring Whether Agents Improve from Their Own Environment Traces

2610.15055 · cs.AI, cs.LG · 2026-10-102610.15055 · cs.AI, cs.LG · 2026-10-10

这篇论文指出自进化 Agent 的评测存在方法学漏洞：常用指标是「更新后的留出成功率减去更新前」，但这个差值可能只是把「冻结的重新打分」当成了进化[30]。做法上提出 Trace Evolution Bench（TEB），把重新打分与真正的上下文或权重更新分开，并给出三个冻结的 80/20/40 数据包；只有验证集不下降、留出标识符绝不进入更新、且文本更新不与权重更新混排时，才接受一次更新；主榜是编码任务，由封闭工作区里的隐藏单元测试评分。结果很扎实：在该数据包上（40 个留出任务、单一模型族），DeepSeek v4 Pro 在 no_evolve 条件下策略完全不变，却在三个随机种子上分别移动了 7.5、5.0 与 12.5 个百分点——这就是评测噪声的量级。进一步，在受 GEPA、ACE 与 ExpeL 启发的九个文本更新单元中，七个超过该种子自身的波动带，其中一个 GEPA 种子反而下降了一个波动带的宽度（-12.5，回归率 0.48）；GLM 与 MiniMax 无法复现这个「九分之七」的模式，没有任何方法在三个模型的所有种子上都稳定高于自身波动带。对做自进化研究的团队，参考价值是报告进化增益时必须同时给出「不更新时的种子波动」，否则无法区分学习与噪声；限制是结论基于单一基准族与三款模型，跨任务族的波动幅度需要另行测量。

This paper identifies a methodological hole in self-evolving agent evaluation: the usual metric is held-out success after an update minus before, and that difference can credit a frozen rescore as evolution[30]. It introduces Trace Evolution Bench (TEB), separating rescoring from a genuine context or weight update, with three frozen 80/20/40 packs; an update is accepted only when validation does not fall, held-out identifiers never enter the update, and text updates are not ranked against weight updates; the primary scoreboard is coding, graded by hidden unit tests in a closed workspace. The results are sobering: on that pack (40 held-out tasks, one model family), DeepSeek v4 Pro under no_evolve never changes its policy yet moves 7.5, 5.0 and 12.5 percentage points across three seeds — that is the scale of evaluation noise. Further, of nine text-update cells inspired by GEPA, ACE and ExpeL, seven exceed that seed's own band, while one GEPA seed falls by a full band width (-12.5, regression 0.48); GLM and MiniMax do not reproduce the seven-of-nine pattern, and no method stays above its own band on every seed across all three models. For self-evolution researchers the reference is to always report the no-update seed band alongside claimed gains. The limit is one benchmark family and three models.

Self-Evolving Visual Agent via Margin Reward

Self-Evolving Visual Agent via Margin Reward

2610.15084 · cs.CV, cs.AI · 2026-10-102610.15084 · cs.CV, cs.AI · 2026-10-10

这篇论文要解决视觉 Agent 训练数据昂贵的问题：让视觉 Agent 学会调用工具通常依赖大量人工标注的问答对或专家演示[31]。做法是 Marvo，一个从无标注图像自进化的方法，由两个 Agent 组成：challenger 从无标注图像生成问答对，solver 通过强化学习解题来学习工具调用；关键设计是一条 margin reward——用 solver「有工具」与「无工具」时的表现差来定义，因为一道好题应当足够难、值得调用工具，同时又仍在 solver 能力范围内，于是 margin 自然形成自适应课程。在覆盖 Agent 推理、视觉搜索、数学与通用现实推理的基准上，该方法优于依赖标注的方案。对做多模态 Agent 的团队，参考价值是「工具带来的边际收益」可以当作自动标注质量的代理指标，用来筛选训练样本；限制是摘要未给出各基准的具体提升数值，且该方法依赖挑战者能生成有效题目，题目分布单一时会退化成自我强化。

This paper tackles the cost of training data for visual agents: teaching a visual agent to invoke tools usually depends on curated question-answer pairs or expert demonstrations at scale[31]. The method is Marvo, a self-evolution approach that learns tool invocation from unlabeled images using two agents: a challenger generates question-answer pairs from unlabeled images, and a solver learns tool invocation by solving them with reinforcement learning. The key design is a margin reward, defined as the solver's performance difference with and without access to tools, since a good training question should be hard enough to warrant tool use yet remain within the solver's reach — the margin yields an adaptive curriculum. Across benchmarks covering agentic reasoning, visual search, mathematics and general real-world reasoning it beats annotation-dependent approaches. For multimodal agent teams the reference is that the marginal benefit of tools works as a proxy for labelling quality when filtering training samples; the limit is that no per-benchmark gains are given, and the method degrades into self-reinforcement if the challenger's questions lack diversity.

Lumen-bench: Cross-Lingual Safety Gaps and the Say-Do Problem in Tool-Calling Large Language Models

Lumen-bench: Cross-Lingual Safety Gaps and the Say-Do Problem in Tool-Calling Large Language Models

2610.15134 · cs.CL, cs.AI · 2026-10-102610.15134 · cs.CL, cs.AI · 2026-10-10

Lumen-bench 追问一个很尖锐的问题：当模型拒绝不道德请求时，这个拒绝意味着什么？在对话里「拒绝」由模型说的话界定，而一旦模型拥有可执行工具，「说的」与「做的」就可能分叉——这是只做英文评测无法揭示的失效模式[32]。做法上构建工具调用伦理的行为基准：覆盖 30 个模型、五种语言（葡语、英语、西语、中文、印地语）、108 个由葡语作者撰写的场景、六种社会压力条件、两种系统提示（伦理提示与中性提示）与 21 个通用工具，共 194,400 次试验；判定由推理模型评审完成，并对照葡语作者校准（n=83，有害/无害 κ=0.78），另与其他两家供应商评审交叉验证（Fleiss' κ=0.78，n=607），辅以沙箱回放。主要发现包括：在伦理提示下，同一模型在印地语与葡语中的有害行动率高于英语（中性提示下为印地语与西语），且经过多重检验校正；以及更强的模型在某些条件下表现未必更安全。对做多语言 Agent 的团队，参考价值是安全评测必须放在「工具可用」的设定下并覆盖用户语言，不能沿用对话式拒绝率；限制是场景由单一语言作者撰写、评审依赖模型加人工校准，跨文化差异的解释仍需后续研究。

Lumen-bench asks a sharp question: when a model refuses an unethical request, what does that refusal mean? In conversation, refusal is bounded by what the model says, but once it holds executable tools, what it does can diverge from what it says — a failure mode English-only evaluation cannot fully reveal[32]. The benchmark is behavioural: 30 models, five languages (Portuguese, English, Spanish, Mandarin, Hindi), 108 Portuguese-authored scenarios, six social-pressure conditions, two system prompts (ethical and neutral) and 21 generic tools, across 194,400 trials, judged by a reasoning-model judge calibrated against one Portuguese-speaking author (n=83; harmful/non-harmful κ=0.78), cross-checked with two other provider judges (Fleiss' κ=0.78, n=607) and complemented by sandbox replay. Findings include higher harmful-action rates in Hindi and Portuguese than English under the ethical prompt (Hindi and Spanish under the neutral prompt), after multiple-testing correction, and that stronger models are not always safer. For multilingual agent teams the reference is that safety evaluation must run in the tool-enabled setting and cover the user's language rather than reusing chat refusal rates; the limit is scenarios authored in one language with model-assisted judging.

Trust Hinders Bidirectional Human-AI Alignment

Trust Hinders Bidirectional Human-AI Alignment

2610.15136 · cs.HC, cs.AI · 2026-10-102610.15136 · cs.HC, cs.AI · 2026-10-10

这篇论文质疑主流的「信任校准」框架：把目标设定为缩小感知可信度与实际可信度的差距、让用户恰当地依赖 AI，这对定义清晰、边界明确的问题有用，但过度限制了「与人一起做决策」的含义[33]。作者提出三个维度：第一，信任 AI 只是管理不确定性的若干策略之一，而不是唯一目标；第二，当 AI 面对「大世界问题」——目标与状态仅部分已知甚至不可知的问题时，信任校准常常被误用；第三，在这类设定里，人机对齐需要处理更高阶的决策过程。文章最后讨论了面向过程的人机耦合系统如何在整个决策任务中共同识别并协商不确定性。对做决策支持产品的团队，参考价值是在目标模糊的场景里，产品目标不该是「让用户更信任模型」，而应是「帮双方把不确定性摊开并协商」；限制是这是一篇立场与方法论文，未提供实证效果，落地到具体产品仍需自行设计交互与评测。

This paper challenges the dominant trust-calibration framing: aiming to close the gap between perceived and actual trustworthiness so users exhibit appropriate reliance is useful for well-defined, clearly scoped problems but overly restricts what deciding with AI means[33]. The authors develop three dimensions: trusting AI is only one of several strategies for managing uncertainty; trust calibration is often misapplied when models face “large-world” problems where goals and states are only partially knowable; and in such settings alignment requires addressing higher-order decision processes. They close by discussing process-oriented human-AI systems that jointly identify and negotiate uncertainty across a task. For decision-support teams the reference is that when goals are ambiguous, the product goal should be surfacing and negotiating uncertainty rather than increasing user trust; the limit is that this is a position and method paper without empirical results.

Structural Chain-of-Thought: Reasoning with Executable Computational Graphs

Structural Chain-of-Thought: Reasoning with Executable Computational Graphs

2610.15107 · cs.CL, cs.AI · 2026-10-102610.15107 · cs.CL, cs.AI · 2026-10-10

这篇论文针对思维链（chain-of-thought）的三个已知缺点：解释难以验证、生成成本高、并且不总是忠实反映模型的真实推理[34]。做法是让模型生成可执行的计算图，而不是自由文本推理：最终答案由确定性执行该图得到；训练用基于图的强化学习，奖励包含结构有效性、答案正确性以及与参考图的节点重合度。在五个基准与四个模型族上的实验显示，该方法稳定优于监督式的图训练，并且与标准 GRPO 与思维链监督训练相比，生成长度最多可缩短 5–6 倍；忠实性实验还显示，用该方法训练的模型更倾向于从生成的图中推导答案，而不是像常规思维链那样事后编造理由。对做推理型 Agent 的团队，参考价值是把推理产物从文本换成可执行结构，同时解决验证与成本两个问题；限制是计算图需要可表达的题型，开放式推理能否形式化为图仍是开放问题，摘要也未给出各基准上的绝对分数。

This paper targets three known weaknesses of chain-of-thought: explanations are hard to verify, expensive to generate and not always faithful to the model's actual reasoning[34]. The method has models generate executable computational graphs instead of free-form reasoning, with the final answer obtained by deterministically executing the graph, trained with graph-based reinforcement learning that rewards structural validity, answer correctness and node overlap with reference graphs. Across five benchmarks and four model families it consistently beats supervised graph training and can cut generation length by up to 5–6x compared with standard GRPO and chain-of-thought supervised training; faithfulness experiments show models trained this way tend to derive answers from the generated graphs rather than producing post-hoc rationalisations as usual. For reasoning-agent teams the reference is that replacing text with an executable structure addresses verification and cost at once; the limit is that graphs need expressible task types, and open-ended reasoning may not be formalisable this way.

Swarm Telepathy: Prefill-Free KV-Cache Transfer Across Families

Swarm Telepathy: Prefill-Free KV-Cache Transfer Across Families

2610.15095 · cs.CL, cs.LG · 2026-10-102610.15095 · cs.CL, cs.LG · 2026-10-10

这篇论文针对多 Agent 系统里最不划算的一环：Agent 之间通过文本通信——一个 Agent 总结发现，另一个 Agent 再把文本读回去，这既把通信限制在离散 token 上，又重复消耗算力[35]。已有工作能做 KV 缓存传输，但要么要求模型同族，要么无法跨上下文、需要接收方重新 prefill 文本。作者的 SwaT（Swarm Telepathy）声称是首个跨模型族的 KV 缓存传输方法，支持多族多 Agent 系统中「免 prefill」的通信：在 Agent 到 Agent 的通信设置里，结合近期的 KV 缓存压缩进展，SwaT 在准确率与速度上都优于文本到文本通信——平均准确率高 40%、墙钟时间快 4 倍，并在 12 个 LLM、6 个模型族的 17 个基准上取得良好表现；此外，当发送方是视觉语言模型时，SwaT 能把视觉能力迁移给纯文本接收方，且超过文本到文本通信。对做多 Agent 系统的团队，参考价值是通信层可能比提示层更值得优化，把「心智状态」直接传递可以同时省时与提质；限制是跨族传输依赖缓存格式与模型内部结构的兼容假设，私有模型与黑盒 API 场景无法使用，安全性（缓存中夹带的信息）也需另行评估。

This paper targets the least efficient link in multi-agent systems: agents communicate through text — one summarises findings, another reads the text back — which confines communication to discrete tokens and burns redundant compute[35]. Existing KV-cache transfer work either requires models within the same family or cannot work across context, forcing the receiver to prefill text. SwaT (Swarm Telepathy) claims to be the first cross-family KV-cache transfer method enabling prefill-free communication in multi-family multi-agent systems: in agent-to-agent settings, combined with recent KV-cache compaction, SwaT beats text-to-text communication in both accuracy and speed — 40% higher accuracy on average and 4x faster wall-clock time — performing well on 17 benchmarks across 12 LLMs from 6 families, and when the sender is a vision-language model it transfers vision capability to a text-only receiver beyond text-to-text communication. For multi-agent teams the reference is that the communication layer may be worth optimising more than the prompt layer. The limit is dependence on compatible cache formats and internals, ruling out closed models and black-box APIs, with cache-borne information leakage still to assess.

To Defer or Not to Defer? Online Learning with LLMs and Congested Experts

To Defer or Not to Defer? Online Learning with LLMs and Congested Experts

2610.15128 · cs.LG, cs.AI · 2026-10-102610.15128 · cs.LG, cs.AI · 2026-10-10

这篇论文处理「模型 + 人类专家」混合系统里的一个实际问题：当专家产能有限时，要不要把这单转给专家，取决于的不只是模型的可靠度，还有专家队列有多拥堵[36]。作者把它形式化为在线序贯决策：每个查询先看到 LLM 答案的置信信号，再决定直接输出还是转给产能受限的专家；难点在于置信信号的可靠性一开始未知、必须从专家反馈中学习，而这种反馈是选择性观测的（只有被转交的查询才会被标注）并且因排队而延迟。方法 KL-PBD 使用未知可靠性参数上的 KL 下置信界来指导转交决策，对 K 个置信档位与时间跨度 T，在每一档准确率都远离转交边界时达到 O(K log T) 的间隙相关遗憾。对做「人在环」Agent 的团队，参考价值是把转交阈值当作需要在线学习的参数，并把「反馈延迟 + 选择性标注」写进建模，而不是用固定阈值；限制是理论保证依赖「准确率远离边界」等条件，且摘要未给出真实业务数据上的对照结果。

This paper addresses a practical issue in LLM-plus-human systems: when expert capacity is limited, whether to defer depends not only on the model's reliability but on how congested the expert queue is[36]. It formulates an online sequential decision problem: for each query the system observes the LLM answer's confidence signal and decides whether to output it or defer to a capacity-constrained expert, with the hard part being that the signal's reliability is initially unknown and must be learned from expert feedback that is both selectively observed (only deferred queries are labelled) and delayed by queuing. The method KL-PBD uses Kullback-Leibler lower confidence bounds on the unknown reliability parameters to guide deferral, achieving O(K log T) gap-dependent regret for K confidence levels and horizon T when accuracy at every level is bounded away from the deferral boundary. For human-in-the-loop agent teams the reference is to treat the deferral threshold as an online-learned parameter and model feedback delay plus selective labelling rather than fixing a threshold; the limit is that guarantees rely on boundary conditions and no real-business comparison is given.
