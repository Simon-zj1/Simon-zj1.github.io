    EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence

    EpiSEM-Bench: Evaluating Safety and Evidence Recovery in LLM Agents under Missing Evidence

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：评测、benchmark）

    The paper targets dangerous execution when a tool omits a critical fact: agents decide whether an action is permitted based on environmental facts, and if a tool response leaves out a safety-relevant fact, the agent may perform a prohibited action even though every returned statement is true and the request is benign. The authors introduce EpiSEM-Bench: 300 matched unsafe/safe task pairs across six domains that systematically suppress critical facts to measure unsafe execution, safety boundaries and evidence recovery. It addresses a failure mode traditional evaluations miss — missing evidence. The limitation is that the benchmark relies on constructed suppression scenarios, and real tool error distributions may differ.

    Causal Chain of Thought: Mitigating Attention Leakage via Context Isolation

    Causal Chain of Thought: Mitigating Attention Leakage via Context Isolation

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：上下文、context）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: 上下文, context)

    论文针对的是思维链可能只是「事后编造」：标准自回归生成中，注意力可以访问完整上下文，模型可能直接从别处查到答案，再生成听起来合理的推理步骤。作者提出 CausalCoT：把问题分解成原子子问题的有向无环图，每个节点只在包含其直接因果父节点的隔离上下文中求解，从而移除注意力泄漏路径。它要解决的是「推理步骤是否真的驱动了答案」这一可解释性核心问题。限制是图分解本身需要构造，复杂任务的分解质量会直接影响结果，计算成本也更高。

    The paper targets chain-of-thought that may be post-hoc: in standard autoregressive generation, attention can reach the full context, so a model may look up the answer elsewhere and then produce plausible-sounding reasoning. The authors introduce CausalCoT, decomposing a problem into a directed acyclic graph of atomic sub-questions, each solved in an isolated context containing only its direct causal parents, removing leakage paths. It addresses the core interpretability question of whether reasoning steps actually drive the answer. The limitation is that the decomposition must be constructed, its quality directly affects results on complex tasks, and compute cost is higher.
