    Learning Self-Orchestration in Decentralized Agent Swarms

    Learning Self-Orchestration in Decentralized Agent Swarms

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：编排、orchestration、multi-agent）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: 编排, orchestration, multi-agent)

    论文针对的是多 Agent 系统里「能力」与「组织」被分开训练的问题：现有系统通常把 Agent 放进固定的编排结构里训练，个体能力学了、协同结构却是人为写死的，于是系统上限由设计者的编排水平决定。作者提出基于市场的后训练（MPT，market-based post-training），让 Agent 用出价（bid）表达参与意愿，出价同时决定「谁来执行」和「集体结果如何转化为局部训练信用」，从而让动作策略与自我编排一起学。它要解决的是去中心化 Agent 群的两难：谁来做事、以及做得好不好该怎么归因到具体成员。效果上的对比基线是固定编排结构下训练的多 Agent 系统；限制是市场机制引入了额外的博弈与不稳定性，出价崩塌或串通都可能让系统退化，论文未给出在超大规模族群下的成本分析。

    The paper targets capabilities and organisation trained separately in multi-agent systems: existing systems typically train agents inside fixed orchestration structures, so individual ability is learned while the coordination structure is hand-written, capping the system at the designer's orchestration skill. The authors propose market-based post-training (MPT), where agents bid to express willingness to participate and bids determine both who acts and how collective outcomes convert into local training credit, letting action policies and self-orchestration be learned together. The problem solved is the decentralised swarm dilemma: who does the work, and how credit for outcomes attaches to specific members. Its baseline is multi-agent systems trained under fixed orchestration; the limitation is that a market mechanism adds game-theoretic instability, where bid collapse or collusion can degrade the system, and the paper gives no cost analysis at very large swarm sizes.

    Improving Environment-Agnostic Coding Agents: The Role of Scaffolding

    Improving Environment-Agnostic Coding Agents: The Role of Scaffolding

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：agent、prompt）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: agent, prompt)

    论文问的是一个很实际的工程问题：如果模型越来越强、能自己构造有用的流程，开发者提供的脚手架（scaffolding）还有多少价值？ 作者用一个基于 Codex 的 Agent 在 ARC-AGI-3、AGI Maze 与 Terminal-Bench 上做对照，两种提示词变体、代码与通用工具完全不变，环境特定信息只作为输入提供。关键发现是：只需修改一条指令——鼓励 Agent 主动报告遇到的困难并把控制权交回以便自动续跑——在两个基准上都能提升平均分，说明「环境无关的通用脚手架」仍有可观的优化空间，不必为每个环境做专门工程。它要解决的是脚手架收益是否已经消失这一疑问，答案是收益仍在，但来源从「环境知识」转向「交互协议」。限制是效果依赖所选基准与单条指令的措辞，能否迁移到真实仓库与更长任务链尚未验证。

    The paper asks a practical engineering question: if models keep getting stronger and can construct useful procedures themselves, how much value does developer-supplied scaffolding still add? The authors compare a Codex-based agent across ARC-AGI-3, AGI Maze and Terminal-Bench using two prompt variants with identical code and general-purpose tools, supplying environment-specific information only as input. The key finding is that changing a single instruction — encouraging the agent to report difficulties and return control for automatic continuation — improves mean scores on both benchmarks, showing that environment-agnostic scaffolding still has real room to improve without per-environment engineering. It resolves the question of whether scaffolding's payoff has vanished: the payoff remains, but its source shifts from environment knowledge to interaction protocol. The limitation is that the effect depends on the chosen benchmarks and the wording of that one instruction, and transfer to real repositories and longer task chains is unverified.

    边界：限制是效果依赖所选基准与单条指令的措辞，能否迁移到真实仓库与更长任务链尚未验证。

    Limits: It resolves the question of whether scaffolding's payoff has vanished: the payoff remains, but its source shifts from environment knowledge to interaction protocol.

    来源：Sources: arXiv

The paper targets an overlooked gap: step-level guards decide whether each tool call is safe before it happens, but users write requests in many languages while guard prompts and the data used to train and evaluate purpose-built guards are English. The authors build LinguaGuardBench from the 1,220 labelled steps of TS-Bench AgentDojo-Traj, translating only the user instruction into German, French, Turkish, Swahili and Amharic while keeping every tool call, observation and label byte-identical, so any change in a verdict is attributable to language alone. It addresses consistency of safety decisions across languages: if a guard systematically loosens or tightens on non-English requests, an attacker only needs to switch language to slip through. Shortcut Sensitivity in Agent-Safety Evaluations

论文针对的是安全评测自身的效度问题：Agent 通过工具行动，其中一部分动作是敏感的，因此守卫必须被可靠地评测；但用于评测守卫的数据里可能混入了与安全概念无关、却能预测标签的捷径线索（shortcut），这些线索还会真实影响守卫的判定。作者提出三级捷径审计框架，区分「标签可预测性」「观测与守卫判定的关联」「配对决策敏感性」，并据此审计 LinuxArena 轨迹与两份 TS-Bench 派生数据。具体发现包括：在 LinuxArena 中更短的平均 shell 命令长度即可强预测轨迹标签；在 AgentDojo-Traj 中精确工具名查找的 F1 从 0.901 下降（即模型的「高分」部分来自记住工具名而非判断安全）。它要解决的是「守卫到底在测什么」这一根本问题。限制是审计结论依赖所选的三个数据集与指标定义，捷径的存在不等于守卫在真实流量上无效。

The paper targets the validity of safety evaluations themselves: agents act through tools, some actions are sensitive, so guards supervising them must be evaluated reliably; yet the data used for that evaluation can contain shortcuts — cues unrelated to the safety concept that still predict labels — and those cues can also influence guard decisions. The authors propose a three-level shortcut-auditing framework distinguishing label predictability, observational guard association and paired decision sensitivity, and audit LinuxArena trajectories plus two TS-Bench-derived step datasets. Specific findings: in LinuxArena shorter average shell-command length strongly predicts trajectory labels, and in AgentDojo-Traj exact-tool lookup falls from F1 0.901 (that is, part of a model's high score comes from memorising tool names rather than judging safety). It addresses the fundamental question of what a guard is actually measuring. The limitation is that the audit depends on the selected datasets and metric definitions, and the existence of shortcuts does not mean a guard fails on live traffic.
