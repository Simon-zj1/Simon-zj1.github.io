    What Does a Harness Repair? A Preregistered Study of Visibility, Baseline Adequacy and Evaluation Defects

    What Does a Harness Repair? A Preregistered Study of Visibility, Baseline Adequacy and Evaluation Defects

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：harness、评测、benchmark）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: harness, 评测, benchmark)

    这篇预注册研究追问 harness 搜索的增益到底从哪来：围绕冻结模型去改提示、推理开关、token 预算或解析器，只要分数涨就保留——但这个涨幅可能来自解析器以前读不出的答案、过弱的对照，或评测本身的缺陷[31]。做法是三个小模型、三个基准、带复制与测试分区的设计、一个 GEPA 搜索臂，并逐一注入六种评测缺陷，完整报告 47 个主要终点。结论很刺眼：关掉 thinking 在 9 个「模型×基准」单元中的 5 个里提高了准确率，而且这些增益主要来自原设定答不出题的样本；在 13 次对比中的 11 次里，关 thinking 与「救援配置」或四个 GEPA 选出的 harness 没有实质差别，只在两个模型的 GSM8K 上输给救援配置；GEPA 确实修好了自己的坏起点，但它选出的 harness 没有一个比关 thinking 更准。另外，在服务引擎里设 thinking 预算（同时允许更长回答）在 9 个单元中的 6 个降低了截断、提高了解析率；在 15 个可评定「缺陷×模型」组合里有 6 个，同一管线的复制反而复现了缺陷造成的扭曲而不是暴露它。对做 Agent 评测与 harness 调优的团队，参考价值是先验「增益是否只是可读性提高」，再宣称 harness 变强；限制是研究规模小（三个小模型、三个基准），作者也强调不要外推到前沿模型。

    This preregistered study asks where harness-search gains actually come from: a change to prompts, reasoning switches, token budgets or parsers around a frozen model is kept if it raises a score, but that gain can come from answers the parser could not previously read, a weak comparison, or a defect in the evaluation[31]. Three small models, three benchmarks, replication and test partitions, a GEPA search arm and six evaluation defects injected one at a time yield all 47 primary endpoints reported. The findings are stark: turning thinking off raised accuracy in 5 of 9 model-benchmark cells, and in each the gain came mostly from questions the capped setting could not answer readably; in 11 of 13 comparisons thinking-off was not meaningfully worse than a rescue configuration or four GEPA-selected harnesses, losing only on GSM8K for two models; GEPA repaired its own broken starting points but none of its harnesses beat thinking-off. A thinking budget in the serving engine lowered truncation and raised parse rate in 6 of 9 cells, and in 6 of 15 evaluable defect-model pairs, replication through the same pipeline reproduced the defect's distortion instead of revealing it. For evaluation and harness-tuning teams the reference is to check whether a gain is only a readability improvement before claiming the harness got better; the limit is the small scale of three small models and three benchmarks, which the authors caution against extrapolating.

    DelegationBench: Measuring When AI Agents Should Ask Before Acting

    DelegationBench: Measuring When AI Agents Should Ask Before Acting

    为什么推给你：Agent 工程（命中：评测、benchmark）

    Why it is here: Agent engineering (matched: 评测, benchmark)

    DelegationBench 检验的是「该不该先问用户」这类评测本身可不可信：这个问题通常被简化成给模型看一个拟执行动作、问它要不要继续，再和人工标签算一致率[32]。基准包含 156 个场景与四种可选回应（直接执行、请求许可、索要缺失信息、拒绝），且多数场景成对出现、只改一个特征：动作是否被要求、代价多大、能否撤销、谁会看到。结论指出一致率会以三种方式误导：一条作者看完基准后写出的关键词规则，比十个模型中的八个更常与标注者一致，可它在 48 对配对场景里只改变 9 次决策；同一个问题的不同问法能让模型执行的频率相差最多 52.5 个百分点；而且所有模型在用工具真正执行任务时，都比单纯评估一个拟执行动作时更少停下来问用户。当规则被明确写出来时，同一批模型几乎完美遵守，说明差距不是「不会守规则」。对做 Agent 授权与确认设计的团队，参考价值是把「一致性、配对敏感性、执行 vs 判断」分开报告，而不是合成一个分数；限制是基准规模有限且标注者偏好会带入文化差异，跨语言与跨行业推广仍需验证。

    DelegationBench tests whether the evaluation of when to ask the user can be trusted at all: the question is usually reduced to showing a model a proposed action, asking whether to proceed, and scoring agreement with human labels[32]. The benchmark has 156 scenarios with four possible responses (act, ask for permission, ask for missing information, refuse), most in matched pairs that change a single feature: whether the action was requested, what is at stake, whether it can be undone, or who will see it. Agreement scores mislead in three ways: a simple keyword rule written after seeing the benchmark agrees with annotators more often than eight of ten models, yet changes its decision in only 9 of 48 matched pairs; equivalent phrasings of the same question change how often a model acts by up to 52.5 percentage points; and every model asks the user less often when it must carry out the task with tools than when judging a proposed action. With rules stated explicitly the same models follow them almost perfectly, so the gaps are not a general inability to follow rules. For teams designing agent permissions and confirmations the reference is to report agreement, paired sensitivity and acting-versus-judging separately instead of one score; the limit is a limited scenario set whose annotator preferences carry cultural assumptions.

    边界：基准包含 **156 个场景与四种可选回应（直接执行、请求许可、索要缺失信息、拒绝）**，且多数场景成对出现、只改一个特征：动作是否被要求、代价多大、能否撤销、谁会看到。

    Limits: the limit is a limited scenario set whose annotator preferences carry cultural assumptions.

这篇预注册研究追问 harness 搜索的增益到底从哪来：围绕冻结模型去改提示、推理开关、token 预算或解析器，只要分数涨就保留——但这个涨幅可能来自解析器以前读不出的答案、过弱的对照，或评测本身的缺陷[31]。做法是三个小模型、三个基准、带复制与测试分区的设计、一个 GEPA 搜索臂，并逐一注入六种评测缺陷，完整报告 47 个主要终点。结论很刺眼：关掉 thinking 在 9 个「模型×基准」单元中的 5 个里提高了准确率，而且这些增益主要来自原设定答不出题的样本；在 13 次对比中的 11 次里，关 thinking 与「救援配置」或四个 GEPA 选出的 harness 没有实质差别，只在两个模型的 GSM8K 上输给救援配置；GEPA 确实修好了自己的坏起点，但它选出的 harness 没有一个比关 thinking 更准。另外，在服务引擎里设 thinking 预算（同时允许更长回答）在 9 个单元中的 6 个降低了截断、提高了解析率；在 15 个可评定「缺陷×模型」组合里有 6 个，同一管线的复制反而复现了缺陷造成的扭曲而不是暴露它。对做 Agent 评测与 harness 调优的团队，参考价值是先验「增益是否只是可读性提高」，再宣称 harness 变强；限制是研究规模小（三个小模型、三个基准），作者也强调不要外推到前沿模型。

DelegationBench 检验的是「该不该先问用户」这类评测本身可不可信：这个问题通常被简化成给模型看一个拟执行动作、问它要不要继续，再和人工标签算一致率[32]。基准包含 156 个场景与四种可选回应（直接执行、请求许可、索要缺失信息、拒绝），且多数场景成对出现、只改一个特征：动作是否被要求、代价多大、能否撤销、谁会看到。结论指出一致率会以三种方式误导：一条作者看完基准后写出的关键词规则，比十个模型中的八个更常与标注者一致，可它在 48 对配对场景里只改变 9 次决策；同一个问题的不同问法能让模型执行的频率相差最多 52.5 个百分点；而且所有模型在用工具真正执行任务时，都比单纯评估一个拟执行动作时更少停下来问用户。当规则被明确写出来时，同一批模型几乎完美遵守，说明差距不是「不会守规则」。对做 Agent 授权与确认设计的团队，参考价值是把「一致性、配对敏感性、执行 vs 判断」分开报告，而不是合成一个分数；限制是基准规模有限且标注者偏好会带入文化差异，跨语言与跨行业推广仍需验证。

DelegationBench tests whether the evaluation of when to ask the user can be trusted at all: the question is usually reduced to showing a model a proposed action, asking whether to proceed, and scoring agreement with human labels[32]. The benchmark has 156 scenarios with four possible responses (act, ask for permission, ask for missing information, refuse), most in matched pairs that change a single feature: whether the action was requested, what is at stake, whether it can be undone, or who will see it. Agreement scores mislead in three ways: a simple keyword rule written after seeing the benchmark agrees with annotators more often than eight of ten models, yet changes its decision in only 9 of 48 matched pairs; equivalent phrasings of the same question change how often a model acts by up to 52.5 percentage points; and every model asks the user less often when it must carry out the task with tools than when judging a proposed action. With rules stated explicitly the same models follow them almost perfectly, so the gaps are not a general inability to follow rules. For teams designing agent permissions and confirmations the reference is to report agreement, paired sensitivity and acting-versus-judging separately instead of one score; the limit is a limited scenario set whose annotator preferences carry cultural assumptions.
