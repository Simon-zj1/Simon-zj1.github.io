论文阅读优化框架

Structured Paper-Reading Framework

这套流程的目标不是把论文压缩成一段摘要，而是把核心结论、证据位置、实验条件、局限和可迁移机制整理成一套能够复核和复用的研究材料。

This workflow is not designed to reduce a paper to another summary. It turns key claims, evidence locations, experimental conditions, limitations and transferable mechanisms into research material that can be checked and reused.

01来源审计Source audit版本、作者、图表完整性Version, authors, completeness
02证据抽取Evidence绑定页码、章节、图与表Bind claims to pages and figures
03结构化深读Deep read问题、方法、实验、边界Problem, method, evidence, limits
04批判复核Critique基线、消融与复现风险Baselines, ablations, reproducibility
05复用输出Reuse笔记、矩阵、汇报与方案Notes, matrix, deck and plan

证据可以回溯

Traceable evidence

重要结论必须回到页码、章节、公式、图或表；无法定位的信息不会被写成确定事实。

Key claims must point back to a page, section, equation, figure or table. Unverifiable information is never promoted to fact.

判断保留边界

Claims within limits

区分作者主张与独立判断，同时检查最强基线、消融、失败案例、成本和复现条件。

Separate paper claims from independent assessment, while checking baselines, ablations, failures, cost and reproducibility.

输出按用途重组

Outputs by purpose

一页卡、深读笔记、多篇矩阵、思维导图和汇报提纲各有不同结构，不是同一段摘要更换格式。

One-page cards, deep notes, multi-paper matrices, mind maps and presentation outlines each use a different structure; they are not one summary in different formats.

好的论文阅读不是把内容变短，而是把结论、证据与适用边界放在一起，让人知道什么可信、还能继续验证什么。

Good paper reading is not about making a paper shorter. It places claims, evidence and boundaries together so we know what is credible and what still needs verification.
